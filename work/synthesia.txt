Yes, you can easily create AI videos with branded backgrounds, colors and logos.

Does AI video creation integrate with existing LMS?
Synthesia's AI video generator is designed to integrate effortlessly with your existing Learning Management System (LMS), ensuring a smooth and efficient workflow. You can create AI videos and directly embed them into your courses and training modules. This seamless integration streamlines the process of updating and distributing training materials.

AI videos created with Synthesia allow companies to be agile and consistent in all types of internal communications and training.
